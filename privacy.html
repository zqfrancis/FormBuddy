FormBuddy Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective November 19, 2026 · FormBuddy by AxisMotion

FormBuddy, developed by AxisMotion, is an on-device motion analysis tool: upload a training video, view skeleton lines and joint angles, and record voice feedback.

Please read the terms below carefully. By continuing to use FormBuddy, you agree to this policy; athletes under 18 must have a parent or guardian read and accept it on their behalf.

1. Default use: on-device, nothing collected

By default, FormBuddy collects, uploads, and shares nothing. Videos, voice feedback, and session records stay in this device's browser (local storage) only — they are never sent to our servers and never sync to other devices. Clearing browser data or deleting a session in the app removes this local data. We require no account, record no name or location, serve no ads, and use no third-party trackers.

Pose analysis runs on your device: the skeletal tracking model processes video frames inside the browser, and no video frame is sent to the model provider.

2. Voluntary sharing: test videos (only if you choose)

The terms in this section apply only if you voluntarily choose to share a training video to help improve FormBuddy; declining does not affect normal use. Shared videos are used strictly for AI body-tracking model calibration, skeletal rendering, data visualization, and coaching feedback.

  • Anonymization: shared videos carry only an anonymous ID (e.g., FB_Alpha_001). No athlete name, location, or other personally identifiable information is attached, and videos are never given to unauthorized third parties.
  • Instructor review: you authorize us to share the anonymized video and data reports only with certified coaches or experts designated for your training evaluation.
  • Retention and deletion: shared videos are retained to keep improving the motion-tracking algorithms. You may contact us at any time to request deletion of a video you shared, and we will delete it within a reasonable time.

3. Minors

FormBuddy is used by athletes under 18. Minors may use the app only with a parent or guardian's consent, and sharing a training video likewise requires a parent or guardian's confirmation. A parent or guardian may contact us at any time to request deletion of their child's shared video.

4. Data security

On-device data is protected by your device and browser; shared videos are encrypted in transit and at rest and accessible only to authorized personnel. We never sell your data.

5. Limitation of liability

FormBuddy is an automated analysis tool to assist athletic training and movement optimization. Its reports do not constitute medical advice, physical therapy, or official safety certification. FormBuddy and its developers are not liable for physical injuries sustained during self-recorded practice or independent training. Please train in a safe environment and within your limits.

6. Changes to this policy

When this policy is updated, its version number increases and the app will show it again on your next launch for you to review and accept.

7. Contact us

For any questions about this policy or your data, or to request deletion, contact FormBuddy (AxisMotion): privacy contact email to be added.
