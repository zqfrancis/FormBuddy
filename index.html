📖 教练使用说明📖 Coach Guide

FormBuddy 是本机动作分析工具：视频、语音点评和录制都只保存在这台设备的浏览器里，不上传服务器，换设备不会同步。FormBuddy analyzes locally: videos, voice feedback and sessions stay in this browser and are never uploaded, so they don't sync across devices.

1. 上传视频1. Upload

学员在学员视图上传一段 15–20 秒的训练视频；教练在教练视图的「学员提交列表」里打开，或自己直接上传。Students upload a 15–20s training video in Student view; coaches open it from the submission list in Coach view, or upload one directly.

2. 录制语音点评2. Record

点 🎙️ 录制语音点评，边播放边讲；讲到关键处暂停，点 📌 抓取定格分析，骨骼力线和关节角度自动标在画面上。Tap Record and talk while the video plays. Pause at a key moment and tap Freeze to mark the frame — skeleton lines and joint angles appear automatically.

3. 圈画和特写3. Annotate

定格时点 ✏️ 画笔，在画面上圈出重点（黄 / 红 / 蓝 / 绿四色）；点 🔍 特写放大关键部位，点画面可以移动焦点。On a frozen frame, use the pen to circle key points (yellow / red / blue / green), and Zoom to magnify the key area — tap the frame to move the focus.

4. 快捷点评4. Templates

录制中点 💬 点评模板，一键插入常用点评（站姿 / 步法 / 拳法 / 踢法 / 防守），也可以在「自定义模板」里加自己的常用语。During recording, tap a feedback template to insert a common comment instantly (stance / footwork / punch / kick / defense), or add your own in Custom Templates.

5. 回放检查5. Replay

点 ▶️ 同步回放，检查语音、字幕、圈画和特写是不是和动作对得上，再决定要不要重录。Tap Play to replay and check that voice, subtitles, annotations and zooms line up with the movement before finalizing.

6. 导出和报告6. Export & report

点 ⬇️ 导出视频，把带语音和标注的视频发给学员；点 📄 报告，看冻结帧截图、角度数据和点评清单，还能一键打印 / 存 PDF。Tap Export to send the annotated video with voice to the student; tap Report for freeze-frame snapshots, angle data and the comment list, printable to PDF.

💡 小提示💡 Tips

  • 录制时视频静音是故意的，防止声音串进麦克风；回放和导出时声音正常。Video is muted during recording on purpose to avoid mic bleed; sound returns in replay and export.
  • 拍 15–20 秒、光线好、能看到全身的视频，分析最准。Best results: 15–20s, well-lit, full-body videos.
  • 每次录制完先回放一遍，确认无误再导出给学员。Always replay once before exporting to the student.
